Methods and boundaries

How results are selected

  • Choose exact task, concurrency and interface family; retain evaluator/workload, adapter, setup, stack and client identities.
  • Cross-provider timing groups require identical workload/evaluator hashes, runtime kind, concurrency and client platform. Requested hardware and placement are shown, not normalised.
  • Multiple compatible-looking configurations are left ambiguous rather than silently averaged or selected as latest.
  • Success intervals belong to individual cohorts. No pooled provider reliability or evidence-confidence score is shown.
  • Incomplete task or requirement evidence becomes a validation route. Missing cost stays unknown; priorities never redistribute missing weights.

What the pilot establishes

Offline wheel tests do not establish native or online package builds. File state is not durable stop/start or Python-global continuity. Timeout responses do not attest remote termination. No serving/GPU/custom-image campaign or invoice-backed cost per successful task was established.
