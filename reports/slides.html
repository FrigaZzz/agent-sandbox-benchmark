20 / Make governance actionable

GDPR is not a blanket
EU-only hosting rule.

EU-only may be your contractual or sector-specific requirement.
International transfers need their own valid basis.

Define the geographic boundary.

EU members, EEA or wider Europe? Include storage and processing.

Check processor and transfer terms.

DPA, subprocessors, remote access, adequacy / safeguards where applicable.

Verify the configured data flows.

Compute, logs, backups, retention, deletion and downstream APIs.

GDPR Articles 28, 32, 44–46. EU compute, encryption or BYOC alone does not establish compliance. The pilot did not attest these controls.

GDPREDPB transfer guide
Presenter notes

Article 28 governs processor guarantees and processing contracts; Article 32 requires risk-appropriate security; Chapter V governs qualifying third-country and onward transfers. Adequacy and safeguards permit qualifying transfers. A third-country processor’s remote access can constitute a transfer even when data stays on EU disks. Foreign ownership alone does not settle the transfer question. Under Article 46 tools, destination-law/practice assessment and supplementary measures may be necessary. See the linked EDPB guidance in the residency research.
