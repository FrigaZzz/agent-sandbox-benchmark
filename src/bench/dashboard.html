Coverage, definitions and collection issues

Pass = expected checks and cleanup succeeded. Fail = an evaluated check or lifecycle requirement failed. Unknown = not validated. Skipped = not exercised, often an adapter or prerequisite gap. Only pass + fail enter the evaluated success denominator; unknown and skipped counts remain visible. Warmups are excluded from comparison rows.

Legacy “live” reports are unvalidated historical observations. A host HTTP executor is not proof of Kubernetes isolation.
